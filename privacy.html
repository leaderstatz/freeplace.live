Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what the Huss An Egg Discord bot ("the bot"), run by the Huss An Egg team ("we", "us"), collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We only use what the bot needs to work.
  • The bot doesn't read or store your normal chat messages.
  • We never sell your data or use it for ads.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

1. What the bot collects

  • Discord account info: your user ID, username, display name and server roles, when you use a command or when the bot manages your roles.
  • Server info: server, channel, role and message IDs, and the server settings the bot manages.
  • What you type into commands: for example, a Roblox username you look up.
  • Roblox info: if you use game commands, your public Roblox info (username, display name and user ID) and your Huss An Egg game stats.
  • Technical logs: errors, and which commands ran and when.

The bot doesn't read, collect or store your regular chat messages.

2. How we use it

  • To run the bot's features and respond to your commands.
  • To keep the server safe and prevent abuse.
  • To find and fix bugs.

We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We only share data with:

  • Discord, because the bot runs on Discord.
  • Roblox, when the bot looks up game info.
  • The hosting provider that runs the bot and stores its data for us.
  • Authorities, if the law requires it.

4. How long we keep it

  • We use what you type into a command to respond to it. We don't store it unless a feature needs it, like a saved setting.
  • Technical logs are deleted after 30 days.
  • Server settings are deleted within 30 days after the bot is removed from a server.

5. Your choices

You can ask what data we have about you, or ask us to delete it, using the contact in section 9. You can stop using the bot at any time. Data held by Discord or Roblox is covered by their own privacy policies.

6. Children

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or older in some countries), and the bot isn't meant for anyone below that age. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Security

We keep the bot's data on secured servers and limit who can access it. No system is perfectly secure, though.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the date at the top of this page and announce big changes in the Huss An Egg Discord server.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, message a staff member in the Huss An Egg Discord server.
